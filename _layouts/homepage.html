Research

Research Interests

Large language models are fluent but not reliable, while symbolic methods are precise but rigid. I study neurosymbolic systems that combine the two: the LLM interprets intent and proposes solutions, and symbolic tools check them, explain what went wrong, and feed that signal back into the model.

  • Learning from symbolic feedback turning failed checks and counterexamples into reinforcement-learning signal.
  • Verifiable synthesis generating access-control policies that SMT-based verifiers check and help repair.
  • Grounded agents agents guided by process models, tool feedback, and repository evidence.
